## Design Specificity Verdict

**Start here.** The homepage first viewport is authored for Nodewise. “Ship a live MVP in 10–14 days” plus “Zero to MVP from ₹29,999. Full source code and ownership transfer,” repeated as four glass HUD cards on a silver sphere, is not a rented agency template. A generic Webflow shop would not put ₹29,999 and full source on frosted telemetry.

Almost everything else is interchangeable. Particle-network wallpaper sits under every route except `/portfolio`. Subpage heroes are one-word titles (Services, Pricing, About, Quality, Contact) over a left-aligned subtitle and a recycled CTA banner. Package copy is stock: Starter Foundation, Growth Engine, Full Architecture, badges Minimum / Standard / Enterprise. About is an inverted logo plus “Lightning-fast, custom-coded platforms.” The footer tagline “Smarter Code. Better Solutions.” could sit on any Indian dev shop.

Worse: the authored offer and the catalog fight. Homepage sells one SKU, then a three-tier menu where Growth Engine (25k+ INR) is visually highlighted under a ₹29,999 flagship. `/packages` never mentions Zero to MVP or ₹29,999. Specificity lasts about one viewport, then the site becomes a leftover agency IA wearing the same glass.

**LLM assessment:** Homepage hero + `/zero-to-mvp` + `/portfolio` are a product. The rest is a thinner company.

**Deterministic scan:** CLI `detect.mjs` on `src/app` + `src/components`: exit 2, **298 findings** (4 warning, 294 advisory). Warnings: `gradient-text` on `.step-num` (`globals.css:1656`); `side-tab` on WhatsApp tooltip triangle and highlighted package top stripe (likely false positives); `design-system-font` Menlo on `.pc-url` (URL chrome fallback). Advisory mass is DESIGN.md ramp tightness: 146 font-size, 131 color, 17 radius. Real signal inside the noise: leftover purple `#7a2ff7`, WhatsApp `#25D366` glow, mixed radius 4/6/8/16px vs 10/12/14/pill.

## Overall Impression

The first screen of `/` is the best thing on the site: a real offer, a real price, a real timeline, and a sculpture that looks like a studio, not a template. The conversion system around it is confused. Pricing hides the flagship. Interior routes are stubs. The proof page (`/portfolio`) is the second-best surface, then it is asked to carry the whole trust load while the homepage CTA “View our work” ships you off-site of the offer. The close (`/contact`) is the sloppiest implementation on the highest-stakes page.

Biggest opportunity: one public SKU map, proof next to the offer, and make every interior page do one job or cut it from the IA.

## What's Working

1. **The offer is spoken in the first viewport.** 10–14 days, ₹29,999, source + ownership are in the H1, the subcopy, and the HUD. `/zero-to-mvp` keeps that honesty with inclusions, exclusions, “from scope confirmation,” and no fake metrics.
2. **`/portfolio` is real Experience work.** Live URLs, named screens, keyboard coverflow, pause, broken-image fallback. Whitebull’s “demo data / live backends removed” is more trustworthy than a fabricated lift stat.
3. **Shared chrome is competent where it exists.** Skip link, labeled hamburger, 44px drawer hits, Escape to close, WhatsApp prefills that name the SKU, live phone/email in the footer.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Four SKUs, three names, Pricing hides the flagship
- **What:** Home: Zero to MVP ₹29,999, then Starter 12k–15k / Growth 25k+ (highlighted) / Enterprise custom. Badges say Minimum / Standard / Enterprise. Titles say Starter Foundation / Growth Engine / Full Architecture. `/packages` does not contain “Zero to MVP” or “₹29,999”. Overlay flagged the badge kickers on the homepage packages block.
- **Why it matters:** The founder’s 5-second job is ₹29,999 / 10–14 days / ownership. The next decision teaches a cheaper highlighted tier exists. They cannot remember which name to send on WhatsApp.
- **Fix:** One public name per SKU. On Pricing, put Zero to MVP first as the timed product. One sentence on how Growth is not the 10–14 day MVP. Stop highlighting Growth if Zero to MVP is the business.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill` (SKU/IA) then `$impeccable clarify`

### [P0] Contact form can double-fire and accepts garbage
- **What:** `ContactForm` posts to FormSubmit. `GlobalInteractions.initContactForm` binds the same `#contact-consult-form` to another POST + `alert()`. `#form-contact` is unvalidated text (`autocomplete="email tel"`). Empty submit in the browser showed only the native bubble. Pitch copy promises WhatsApp, email, or a brief; the panel shows `tel:+919446998827` with no spaces and no WhatsApp/email in the card. The green FAB covers the submit corner.
- **Why it matters:** Highest-stakes close. Duplicate leads or a browser `alert()` after a cinematic homepage destroys trust.
- **Fix:** Delete `initContactForm`. Validate email-or-phone. One success state. Put WhatsApp + `contact@nodewise.cc` in the pitch. Restyle chips off purple. Keep the FAB off the submit button.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

### [P1] Homepage decides before it proves, then over-asks
- **What:** Primary CTA is View our work, but no work is on the page. Desktop: 3 in-hero CTAs + floating WA. Mobile: copy → 3 full-width buttons → cramped sculpture → bullets that used to support the decision. Overlay: the HUD that carries price/timeline/ownership is 9px type.
- **Why it matters:** DESIGN.md wants proof-first. A founder who needs proof must leave the offer. Casey taps the first large button or the green circle. The sculpture’s facts are the product and they fail the 11px floor.
- **Fix:** Keep View our work as the filled button. Put a single strip of the four live project names (or one Whitebull still) under the CTAs. Two actions on mobile; consult as text. Move bullets above CTAs. Raise HUD type to ≥12px.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout` / `$impeccable typeset`

### [P1] Interior routes are stubs that leak out of the header
- **What:** `/capabilities` is a 3-card carousel so two services are off-stage. `/process` is three one-line steps (HowTo JSON-LD lists four; Architecture is missing). `/about` is a logo box + founder names with no roles. `/quality` is 01–03 generic columns; “No page builders. Source is yours.” is the one true line. Process / Services / Quality are footer-only. Mobile drawer peeks the page behind a partial-width sheet and still omits those routes.
- **Why it matters:** After a specific home, this reads as a different, thinner company. Discoverability is accidental.
- **Fix:** Cut them from the product IA, or give each one job: three visible capability cards mapped to SKUs; process as a 10–14 day week map; about as two humans with one sentence each; quality led by ownership / no page builders.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill` / `$impeccable shape`

### [P1] Experience and close chrome fight the work
- **What:** Portfolio overlay: 37 hits, including clipped coverflow, 10.4px chrome labels, and text occlusion on URL/window chrome. Mobile portfolio: large dead space, Visit overlapping the screenshot, WhatsApp covering body copy. Contact FAB overlapping Submit. Particles on thin interior pages. Undismissable 60px green orb on every route, including ZTM (generic “custom inquiry” prefill vs the SKU prefill on the page CTA).
- **Why it matters:** `/portfolio` is the proof surface. If it feels heavy, clipped, or like homework (`titan-bigs.vercel.app`, `mavenixstudio.netlify.app`), the primary homepage CTA fails. Casey’s thumb prefers the orb to the offer.
- **Fix:** Recess coverflow chrome; raise labels; don’t occlude card type. Idle or kill particles off home. On `/contact` and `/zero-to-mvp`, either drop the FAB or match its prefill to the page offer. Pad submit away from the orb.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable polish` / `$impeccable quieter`
