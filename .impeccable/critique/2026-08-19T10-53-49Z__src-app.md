## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM:** Authored for Nodewise on the homepage hero (sculpture + glass offer facts) and `/portfolio` coverflow. Category-interchangeable everywhere else: dark glass agency IA, generic tagline, inverted favicon wordmark. A Kochi studio could swap the name and most inner pages would still read.

**Deterministic scan:** Detector exit 2, 298 findings (4 warning, 294 advisory). Real warnings: gradient-text on `.step-num`. Two `side-tab` hits are false/misclassified (tooltip triangle; package hairline). Advisory mass is DESIGN.md type/color coverage noise, not 294 defects.

**Visual overlays:** No reliable user-visible overlay. Browser mutation/injection was not performed (read-only evaluate only). Fallback: CLI detector + a11y snapshot at 1280 and 320.

## Overall Impression

The studio already has the only commercial line that matters: ₹29,999, 10–14 days, source and ownership. The site keeps interrupting it. Conversion is a WhatsApp tap from price, not a paid close from trust.

## What's Working

1. Commercial honesty: fixed price, timeline, inclusions, exclusions, INR transparency.
2. Hero instrument: glass HUD chips are the offer, not decoration; mobile copy-then-CTAs-then-stage is the right stack.
3. WhatsApp is a real Indian close path with package-specific prefills; ZTM sticky rail is the strongest buy object.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Flagship offer missing from Pricing; Growth is the highlighted SKU**
- Why: PRODUCT.md conversion is Zero to MVP. `/packages` never shows ₹29,999. Home visually recommends 25k+ Growth beside it.
- Fix: Put Zero to MVP on Pricing as the featured SKU. Demote SMB packages.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable shape` then `$impeccable distill` on `/` and `/packages`

**[P0] Primary CTA is proof, and the proof does not close**
- Why: Solid pill is “View our work.” Portfolio is demo data, Vercel/Netlify hosts, a college club, a sibling studio landing.
- Fix: Make “Start your MVP” primary. Relabel or cut cases that cannot survive an investor screenshare.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable distill` (IA/CTA) + `$impeccable clarify` (portfolio framing)

**[P1] Two products, one coat**
- Why: Home/ZTM/portfolio were redesigned; Capabilities/Process/Quality/About/Contact are old agency skeleton with glass `!important`.
- Fix: Either finish the visual world on those routes or strip them to a thin convert-or-proof IA.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable document` then `$impeccable quieter` / `$impeccable layout` on inner routes

**[P1] Dual WebGL + per-frame GSAP (TBT ~3s, TTI ~26s on throttled mobile)**
- Why: Hero sculpture plus particle field (`gsap.to` inside rAF). Motion delays comprehension.
- Fix: Pause/hide particles when the hero is in view; never `gsap.to` per frame; respect `prefers-reduced-motion` everywhere.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable optimize`

**[P1] WhatsApp is the close; Contact is a Western form**
- Why: Header says Contact. FormSubmit + combined email/phone + purple chips. Error says WhatsApp with no link. FAB prefill is generic.
- Fix: WhatsApp-first contact with the same ZTM prefill; form as fallback.
- Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify` + `$impeccable harden`
