## Users

Primary: B2B companies shopping website engineering and custom web applications on published INR packages.

## Product Purpose

Nodewise is an engineering studio that builds B2B websites, landing pages, and web applications. Success is a buyer who understands published INR pricing, trusts ownership transfer, and starts via WhatsApp or a brief.

## Positioning

B2B websites engineered in Next.js (from ₹20,000 INR, no retainer) and web applications (₹1–1.5 lakh; custom platforms quoted). India studio, worldwide delivery. No WordPress, no page builders.

## Operating Context

Buyers evaluate on mobile and desktop marketing site (nodewise.cc), often comparing studios before a WhatsApp inquiry. Contact via WhatsApp (+91 94469 98827), email (contact@nodewise.cc), and LinkedIn. Primary conversion path: homepage → packages (websites / web apps) → WhatsApp or contact.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Builds: B2B websites, landing pages, web applications, portals, dashboards, light automation.
- Offers: Websites from ₹20,000 INR (3 pages: Home, About, Enquiry; extra pages priced in the calculator with no cap; Next.js on Vercel; no maintenance fee). Web applications ₹1–1.5 lakh INR. Custom platforms quoted.
- Site stack: Next.js, React, Vercel. Application stack: Node.js, Next.js route handlers, PostgreSQL, Supabase Auth, REST APIs. Platforms add Python, WebSockets, RAG, Gemini/Mistral, realtime voice, custom models.
- Stack in production: Next.js 14, React 18, GSAP, Three.js, Vercel Analytics.
- Routes: /, /portfolio, /packages, /website-development, /software-development, /about, /contact. /capabilities and /services redirect to /packages. /process and /quality redirect to /about.
- Preserve published prices, inclusions, exclusions, WhatsApp, email, and founders. Homepage leads with websites and web applications. No Zero to MVP offer.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the B2B job: websites and web applications with published INR prices.
2. Never invent commercial claims; keep published prices, inclusions, and contact paths exact.
3. Speed and ownership are the trust signals. Show them, do not bury them.
4. Premium craft is allowed; it must not obscure offer clarity or primary actions.
5. Motion explains product quality and continuity; it must not delay comprehension or conversion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect prefers-reduced-motion; keep content readable without WebGL; keyboard-accessible controls and links; sufficient contrast on dark UI.
